ABOUT FAITH

Country roots.
A love for old things.

I’ve always loved the simple beauty of farmhouse and cottage living. Growing up surrounded by country roots gave me an appreciation for old things, cozy spaces and the stories that come with pieces from another time.

Thrifting, antique finds and the unique character in every home have always been part of what I love. Saddle & Stone brings that together with helping families through important transitions — thoughtfully organizing each sale, creating inviting displays and helping treasured pieces find their next home.

My goal is to make the process feel personal, respectful and beautifully handled from the first walk-through to the final day of the sale.
