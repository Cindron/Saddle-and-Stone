ABOUT ME

Country roots.
A love for old things.

I’ve always had a love for the simple beauty of farmhouse and cottage living. Growing up surrounded by country roots, I developed an appreciation for old things, cozy spaces and the stories that come with pieces from another time.

I’ve always loved thrifting, antique finds and the unique character in every home. Through Saddle & Stone, I get to combine that passion with helping others — creating thoughtful estate sales and giving treasured items a new chapter.
